# Policy impact feedback

Policy actions retain their original numerical model and five-second duration. The scene now connects the policy location to affected water, habitat, seagrass, coral or delivered-power regions. Animated rings and numeric labels show actual bounded before/current values; positive and negative changes use both direction symbols and color. Actions with no numerical effect explicitly say that environmental conditions have not improved. External events remain separate and are not depicted as policy achievements.

The dashboard distinguishes current change from the completed effect, while the action area summarizes signed final changes. Greater sediment contrast and more distinct living-coral colors support the same continuous water and health values. No new temperature, carbon or emissions scores are invented.

A bilingual climate explanation appears beside the scene and near the top of the automatic result dialog. Coral policy does not cool a heatwave; seagrass indices do not establish carbon credits; increased wind delivery can displace fossil generation but does not quantify emissions savings. Existing caveats and heat-bleaching persistence remain.

Verification includes all 18 policy/response marker combinations, 54 outcome routes, model-to-display reconciliation, and isolated desktop/phone playthroughs. QA cloud writes are disabled.
